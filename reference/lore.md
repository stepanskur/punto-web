## Check-In service: Drop Off Your Baggage at Belorussky Station for Free! 🎒🚉✈️

We are making your journeys even more seamless! In partnership with **Aeroexpress**, punto.fly is launching an exclusive service for passengers departing from Moscow **Sheremetyevo (SVO)**.

Say goodbye to dragging heavy suitcases through the city - now you can check in your luggage right at **Belorusskiy Railway Station** before boarding the train!

**How does it work?**
Drop off and tag your bags at the dedicated punto.fly desk at the station.
Your luggage is securely transported straight to the airport.
Travel light, enjoy your Aeroexpress ride, and skip the airport check-in lines.
Collect your bags at your final destination!

**Key Privileges:**
🆓 100% Free - This service is completely complimentary for all punto.fly passengers across all fare tiers (from Basic to Business).
🔄 Full Connection Support - Flying with a layover at Sheremetyevo (e.g., connecting to our brand-new Zurich hub)? Your baggage will be automatically transferred to your connecting flight.

Your journey now begins at the train station.
